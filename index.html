Loopnaro Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Loopnaro respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Loopnaro mobile game.

Information Stored on Your Device

Loopnaro may store game preferences and statistics locally on your device, including:

  • Sound settings
  • Music preference
  • Vibration settings
  • Theme preference
  • Game statistics such as games played, wins and losses

This information is used to provide game functionality and is not uploaded to our own servers.

You can remove locally stored information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling Loopnaro.

Advertising

Loopnaro uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • Device and advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • General location information derived from the IP address
  • App interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information used for advertising, analytics, security and fraud prevention

Google processes this information according to its own privacy policies and advertising requirements.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, Loopnaro may request your consent before requesting certain types of advertising.

Users in regions with applicable privacy regulations may be given options to manage their advertising and privacy choices.

Where available, these choices may also be reviewed again through privacy settings provided inside the app.

Accounts and Online Services

Loopnaro currently does not require a user account.

The current version does not use our own cloud database to store player profiles, game statistics or game progress.

Data Sharing

Loopnaro itself does not sell personal information.

Information processed through Google AdMob may be shared with Google and its advertising partners as necessary to provide advertising, measurement, security and related services.

Data Retention

Game preferences and statistics stored locally remain on the device until they are changed, cleared or the application is uninstalled.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google is retained according to those services' own policies.

Security

We take reasonable steps to minimize the information handled directly by Loopnaro.

Third-party services used by the app apply their own security measures and privacy practices.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Loopnaro's features, advertising systems, online functionality or legal requirements change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
